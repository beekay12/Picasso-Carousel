Pablo Picasso

Six paintings, from the Blue Period to Guernica

The painter

Pablo Picasso grew up in Spain and began painting as a child with encouragement from his father, a drawing teacher. He studied art in A Coruña and Barcelona before making his first visit to Paris in 1900.

The paintings here span more than three decades, from the restrained blues of The Old Guitarist to the fractured figures of Guernica. Between those works, Picasso and Georges Braque developed Cubism, breaking familiar subjects into overlapping planes.
