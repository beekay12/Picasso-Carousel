# 🎨 Pablo Picasso — A Life in Paint

An interactive gallery of six Picasso paintings, from the Blue Period to *Guernica* and *The Weeping Woman*. A full-width black-and-white portrait opens the exhibition and fades to black at a thin gold divider. Browse the works, open a painting for a closer look, and listen to its audio guide.

## 🖼️ Explore the gallery

- Filter the carousel by period and navigate with swipes, on-screen arrows, or your keyboard.
- Select **View painting** for a larger image, artwork details, and an audio guide.
- Keep listening while you browse. Pause or resume from the painting card, the detail view, or the playback control.
- Watch the SVG waveform respond to the narration. The gallery respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Read a short artist biography and discover one of 14 sourced Picasso quotes on each page load.

## 🚀 Run locally

Download or clone the repository, keep the files together, and open `index.html` in your browser. The gallery uses HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript and runs without a build step.

## 📁 Project structure

- `index.html` contains the gallery markup, styles, and interactions.
- `images/artworks/` contains the six paintings.
- `images/artist/picasso-portrait.avif` is the hero portrait. The page applies the grayscale treatment and fade in CSS.
- `audio/` contains the MP3 guides and precomputed waveform data.
- `picasso-cartoon.ico` is the pixel-art tab icon.
